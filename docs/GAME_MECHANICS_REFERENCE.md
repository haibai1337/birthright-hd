## Balance implications

Guaranteed development rewards planning but grows income rapidly; caps and military upkeep are deliberate counterweights. Guild/temple income is simplified to ceil(level/2) rather than the original multi-axis collection table. Class restrictions on holding income are intentionally absent, matching the PC distinction between income and class-specific regency collection. Source investments compete with province growth.

Peace and alliances are transparent threshold decisions instead of hidden random rolls. The current diplomacy model can improve relations through repeated offers and is not a sophisticated negotiation simulation. All regents receive the same initial 24 GB/20 RP and three actions. Each starts with four provinces and two infantry; unit minimums and economic conditions still matter.

Auto-combat destroys losing units, so failed offensives are severe. Reinforcing before an attack matters. No siege, retreat, morale, commander bonus, experience, or monthly army movement is claimed. The combat adapter allows those systems to replace the formula later.

CLASSIC currently means a configuration profile with verified tax/unit/regency foundations and seasonal war behavior. It is not the complete 1997 game. Do not relabel remaining enhanced/placeholder systems as classic without supporting evidence and regression tests.
