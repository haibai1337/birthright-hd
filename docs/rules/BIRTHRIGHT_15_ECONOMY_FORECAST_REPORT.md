## Implemented calculation contract

`packages/core/economy15.ts` is the exact, read-only companion to the existing economy/report API, exported through the core index. The legacy `economicForecast`/`forecast` functions remain numeric and guarded. Callers explicitly select `economicForecast15` or `economicForecasts15` with a Birthright 1.5 foundation; legacy rules identity is rejected. No UI or automatic dispatch/activation was added.

```ts
const options = { overlays: matchingCharacterAndHandlerContent };
const domains = economicForecasts15(foundation, scenario, options);
const domain = economicForecast15(foundation, scenario, realmId, options);
```

Attached overlay references require actual matching content. Missing or mismatched referenced content fails verification; it is not silently replaced. A null-overlay foundation can still quote fixed cash, keeping all unqualified virtual cash conditional. Original scenario identity and overlay identity remain separate.

The money path uses branded BigInt quantities, **120 units/GB**, including huge treasuries, obligations, sums, differences, transfers and discounts. Bounded levels, die results and skill modifiers remain numbers; balances are never projected to Number. Static profile prices are converted losslessly at the accounting boundary. JSON support files encode exact quantities as canonical integer strings; CSV uses exact fractional GB. No campaign/save format changed in this increment.

Implemented amounts:

- Province P plus an explicitly authorized whole-GB tax modifier, capped at +50% of base, with revenue floored at zero. This quotes an existing approved modifier; it creates no Decree or persistent tax state. The default is zero, not a reinterpretation of legacy dice-tax choices. Existing taxable-control/loyalty and occupation entitlement is reused, preventing a second sovereign payment.
- Active Law H/3, Guild/Temple 2H/3, Source zero and qualified virtual Guild 2·max(H−4,0)/3. Holdings are aggregated by owner/province/type before formulas; all record/source IDs remain. Contested/destroyed levels are inactive. Imported excess levels are retained. Occupation alone does not silently suppress independent institutions.
- Seaport P and river port P/2, retaining existing disruption rules and independent recorded port ownership. No added ordinary port bill, virtual trade endpoint, speculative route income, trade RP, ordinary Law Claims, extraordinary Seizure or generic holding-count administration.
- Each actual unit's represented native/static profile upkeep, independent of current strength and legacy payroll selection. Missing profiles are unpriced diagnostics, never invented muster-derived prices. The four costless deployed monster-profile cases retain recorded zero and an uncertainty qualification.
- One GB per supported active fort **asset**, including towers, independent of level and province fort summaries. Current independent fort ownership remains authoritative. A fort summary without asset identity is diagnosed rather than billed twice.
- Court C costs C GB, with opening tier 1 mapping to 1. `courtLevels` can quote an explicit derived C=5+ projection without changing the legacy court-tier save schema, progression, seat/palace capacity or action slots.
- Supported, paid, separately priced existing infrastructure and recorded contracts supplied as evidence-bearing `infrastructure` facts. Unclassified and hypothetical construction remain separate; absent prices never become fabricated bills. Bare road edges have no invented upkeep/payer.
- Existing recorded lieutenant salaries, foreign occupation costs and GB tribute obligations. Unknown salaries remain diagnostics. Additional evidenced `commitments` distinguish outstanding, reserved and already-paid amounts; paid charges are not billed again. Duplicate bills/fort assets and separate ordinary port charges are rejected.

The shared, unchanged NPC eligibility predicate now accepts balance-independent identities and is reused by legacy and exact forecasts: AI control, original index 1–34, the explicit 17/19/23/24/26/29 set, and no direct/chained player control. Management mode does not affect qualification or price.

`tradeEndpointQuote15` and `TradeEndpointQuote15` prepare exact H/2 positive-real-Guild endpoint shares. They are **amount quotes only**, not proof of bilateral consent, transport, capacity or an executable route. Existing legacy routes lacking that contract evidence are visible as unresolved, with no speculative receipt. Increment 6 owns execution and contract integration.

## Administrate and funding quotes

The forecast assembles eligible costs only from supported forts and separately priced, paid, supported roads/bridges/palaces. It excludes payroll, court, ports, other infrastructure, ordinary embedded holding administration, taxes, action fees, magic and obligations. `administrateQuote15` accepts an already eligible typed cost snapshot and a named nomination; the complete forecast builds that snapshot from the represented assets. Military/court categories cannot enter that typed quote.

Actor review/authority comes from `characterActor35`; the modifier comes from `skillCheck35`. There is no maximum-rank or incomplete-sheet fallback. With no supplied die result, the quote reports eligibility, exact DC and potential saving but applies **zero** reduction. With an explicitly supplied d20 result it compares exactly against `10 + eligible gross GB`, preserving half-rank modifiers and fractional DCs. Success saves `floor(eligibleGB/4)` whole GB once; per-asset reductions use exact ticks/largest remainders with stable ties. One nomination per domain is permitted, and `alreadyApplied` prevents an additional quoted reduction. Neither quoting a die nor viewing the result consumes RNG or persists a seasonal success. Increment 8 must supply the committed, once-only result/cursor.

Recorded GB transfers receive conserved funding quotes using opening cash plus ordinary production, less explicit reserved commitments, **before any incoming same-boundary transfer**. An unfunded circular promise or incoming relay cannot create funds. Partial funding is apportioned in ticks with stable contract-ID ties; counterpart credits equal quoted debits. Each side retains source/counterparty identity, quoted payment and unfunded balance. These quotes do not execute payments or choose nonpayment consequences.

`result.netOperating` and signed `projectedClosing` include all known due obligations. `cashAfterQuotedTransfersAndOtherBills` separately reflects the funded-transfer preview. `unfundedTransfers` and `otherCashShortfall` expose the funding gap without counting an unpaid transfer twice. Costs whose prices are missing remain explicit unpriced source IDs/diagnostics rather than invented numeric shortfalls. The final order of real allocations/defaults and paused receipts belongs to Increment 8; these forecast keys are not completed receipts.
