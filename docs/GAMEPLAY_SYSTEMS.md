# Gameplay systems

Anuire is the only application campaign. It contains 206 provinces, 51 domains and 116 original regent records, with 18 currently selectable regents. Active territorial and holding domains use the same deterministic command validation as the player. With a territorial player, 48 domains use AI and two unused/placeholder domains remain passive.

Each season has three domain action rounds in a seeded regent order. After a regular player action, other domains take their slots before the player's next action. Mustering, marching and the priest's first Agitate are free orders. End season passes remaining player slots and advances seasonal accounting; an RP defense request pauses for the player's response. Manual saves, autosaves and exported/imported JSON saves are supported.

## M1B-4 — Realm magic, ley lines and bloodlines

**Evidence and recovery.** CD `RULES.TXT` §§016/231, 043/125, 147 and 159 take priority over the modified historical implementation at commit `fc47055002c4b776bfb1e8737b5aaf7f6d43edce`: [ACTNMENU.CPP](https://github.com/fire-lizard/birthrt/blob/fc47055002c4b776bfb1e8737b5aaf7f6d43edce/ACTNMENU.CPP), `RLMSPELL.CPP/HXX`, `INVNTORY.CPP`, `PLAYSTAT.CPP`, `PLAYCLAS.HXX`, `REALM.HXX` and `BLOOD.CPP`. The manual delegates detailed mechanics to the in-game help. These are research inputs; no historical binary was executed or source implementation shipped.

`npm run research:magic` reads original `SCENES/IDnnnn.INV` inventories (comments and DOS EOF supported) and source-place route fields, writing private `reference/extracted/anuire/magic.research.json`. It recovers **82 inventories for 116 regents**, containing **114 realm-spell items**, **118 blood-ability items of 16 types**, and **five original ley lines belonging to Ilien**. Ley references identify source _place indices_, unlike guild route province references. Import verifies ownership, source types and campaign-template SHA-256. Missing inventories stay unknown. Priest baseline spells granted by `AddPriestSpells` are added by the same class/level rules; wizard books are never randomly filled. Asset import regenerates and serves the sidecar. All 206 provinces, 51 domains, 766 holdings, original selection flags and scenario fingerprint remain preserved.

**Bloodlines and RP.** Original derivation, grade and numerical strength remain separate from stored RP. AVA character classes/levels now override strategic class codes where the historical loader does so, preserving raw originals and special-NPC exceptions. The previous strategic mapping incorrectly treated thief code 6 as wizard; this is corrected. Multiclass regents receive each eligible contribution once: territorial levels; full law for fighter/paladin/ranger, half rounded up for priest/thief; full guild for thief/ranger, half rounded up for bard; full temple for priest/paladin; full source for wizard; trade RP for thieves. Contested/destroyed holdings and occupied provinces contribute no relevant RP. Collection is limited by current bloodline strength, independently of stored reserves. New receipts stop at the native unsigned-16-bit limit **65,535 RP**; larger existing saves retain their balances. The contradictory historical setter overflow/reset is not reproduced. A free **Increase bloodline strength** order buys one point for current strength + 1 RP, up to the original UI ceiling 99; higher imported scores survive. Recovered blood abilities are displayed, but their tactical/RPG effects, inheritance and bloodtheft remain unresolved.

**Sources and ley networks.** M1B-2 Create/Rule/Contest remains the only source-development system, including civilization/elven potential and preservation of imported excess. Sources belong to their holding owner regardless of territorial conquest or occupation. Casting needs an eligible class and one's own uncontested local source/temple; ownership alone grants no spellcasting. Wizard/magician/bard classes can supply arcane casting; only wizard/magician can forge ley lines. Priests use local temples, never ley power. Dual-class eligibility is checked per spell school; the historical menu's first-caster-class preference is not imposed.

A ley line joins two owned sources, including level zero, across real directed province borders; foreign sovereignty needs no alliance. It costs one GB and one RP per traversed edge, plus optional offensive RP, and consumes an action. The shared M1B-2 auction handles endpoint sovereign opposition and seeded d20 against `5 − origin level − bid + opposition`; spent costs remain spent on failure. Three outgoing slots per source and duplicate-pair restrictions match recovered structures. Connected sources share their **highest level, never their sum**; disconnected holdings supply nothing remotely. Contest suspends connections, restoration reactivates them, destruction is terminal, and occupation leaves them operational. Ownership mismatch suspends rather than transfers a line. No recurring upkeep was verified. **Compatibility choices:** shortest directed paths replace the historical greedy coordinate-distance walk; contest/ownership suspension and generic opposition AI are explicit policies rather than certified retail behavior.

**Spell catalogue.** Thirteen of the 18 recovered inventory entries execute real effects (the two Dispel entries share an effect). Automatic spells consume one action and their quoted resources without an invented success roll or RP auction. Targets need local magical access; supported area extensions use original adjacency and caster-level limits.

The Investiture inventory entry directs players to M1B-1 investment rather than bypassing occupation. No verified strategic spell-research action was found; learned books persist but new acquisition remains deferred. Historical `Subversion` has no corresponding recovered inventory entry and is not invented.

Automatic highest-level-first mundane holding reduction and Bless Army's mapping onto the current combat resolver are compatibility implementations. Blood-ability domain modifiers, exact retail resistance/area edge cases, summons and RPG acquisition need further investigation.

**Playing and persistence.** Realm council contains bloodline, casting, active effects and ley controls with eligibility, resource quotes and unavailable reasons. Existing parchment layout is retained. AI uses a small validated set of beneficial spells and source links. Version-1 saves gain optional fields without replacing IDs, resources, economy or warfare state; missing books/verified initial links upgrade from matching scenario data. Destroyed referenced source tombstones survive for ley provenance. Covenant Marches retains its legacy magic policy.

**Validation:** all 10 focused magic tests and 35 M1B-1/2/3 regression tests passed. TypeScript and the browser production build passed, including the complete original-asset copy. An isolated Chromium smoke at 1366×768 completed ley forging → Alchemy with GB/action accounting → save → priest Bless Land → seasonal expiry, on the original 206-province/51-domain/766-holding campaign, without browser errors. Only the magic file was rechecked after its initial save-normalization correction; no repeated full suite, Tauri or Rust build was run.

## M1B-3 — Economy and trade

**Evidence.** CD `RULES.TXT` §§097/099, 110–112/167, 116/250, 126–129/168 and 133–135 are authoritative. Dice distributions and implementation details are corroborated against historical [PROVTAX.C](https://github.com/fire-lizard/birthrt/blob/fc47055002c4b776bfb1e8737b5aaf7f6d43edce/PROVTAX.C), [GAMEMAP.CPP](https://github.com/fire-lizard/birthrt/blob/fc47055002c4b776bfb1e8737b5aaf7f6d43edce/GAMEMAP.CPP) and [ACTNMENU.CPP](https://github.com/fire-lizard/birthrt/blob/fc47055002c4b776bfb1e8737b5aaf7f6d43edce/ACTNMENU.CPP). That modified source is not proof of stock behavior. The manual delegates detailed domain rules to in-game help; no patch evidence overrides these economic tables. No historical executable was run.

**Revenue.** Provincial taxes now roll the original dice, including negative modifiers clamped to zero; level 1 light tax averages **1/3 GB**, not 1/2. Guild/temple collection uses both province and holding level and seeded dice, replacing `ceil(level/2)`. Law has no automatic GB and sources never produce GB. Contested/destroyed holdings earn neither their holding income nor holding RP. Occupiers alone receive eligible provincial taxes, neither party receives provincial RP, and the occupier pays 1 GB/province. Pillage continues to lower the province's level and loyalty. Tax/occupation loyalty adjustments are queued for the following seasonal boundary; active law or occupying units mitigate negative changes. The CD's normal tax changes take precedence over the source's additional low-law penalty.

**Maintenance.** Replace one GB per owned province with the original bracket table, counting owned provinces plus positive, surviving law/guild/temple holdings (source implementation excludes sources and level-zero records). Each independently owned positive castle/tower costs 1 GB, regardless of level. Courts cost **0/1/3/6/9 GB**; initial decoded court level is minimal (1) for all 51 records. Court changes are free and no court prevents financial/non-war diplomacy. Original percentage diplomacy modifiers are known, but applying them awaits replacement of the existing compatibility negotiation model in M1B-5. Native troop IDs/combat balance remain intact: only three generic unit upkeep values are verified; imported native-type upkeep remains explicitly provisional. A raw bitmap/type code must not be used blindly as an index into the source's unit-upkeep table.

**Trade.** In **Realm council → Trade routes**, select your origin guild, an eligible destination, and optional RP bid. Creating land trade costs **1 GB + 1 RP + bid, one action**, with a seeded d20 threshold of `10 − guild level − bid + opposition`. Local origin guild/law owners can oppose through the existing saved RP auction. The destination requires no guild. Endpoints need different terrain; all guild routes together consume each endpoint's capacity: levels 1–3: one, 4–6: two, 7+: three (three-slot source cap). Routes require continuous **directed** roads through own/allied destination provinces; original movement codes **8/9** identify the **202** recovered road edges. River-only code 7 is insufficient. No geometric connections or reverse borders are invented. Existing road construction controls use adjacent owned/allied provinces, the CD's summed terrain costs and high-mountain restriction, as a free order. Built roads are saved separately from original geography.

Route income is `floor((level A + level B)/2)`. Thieves receive trade RP before the bloodline cap, rounding combined trade receipts down once to whole RP. Occupation, contest, lost passage and reduced capacity suspend revenue; recovery restores it. Guild destruction destroys its routes; territorial conquest does not transfer a guild or its routes. A guild ownership change carries its routes with it. Ending one's route is a free **compatibility** control; tombstones persist. New sea routes are prohibited by the PC help.

**Recovered economic assets.** `npm run research:economy` reads `RLMSTART.DAT` without modifying or executing it and writes private `reference/extracted/anuire/economy.research.json`, also regenerated by asset import. Its SHA-256 must match the campaign source. It recovers **19 ports**, **51 court records**, **171 guild records (all 513 route fields zero)** and the **52×52 tribute matrix (all zero)**. **User-approved HD income rule:** all 11 seaports earn full province-level GB, including Jogh Warren (Gorgon's Crown): **3 GB/season** at starting level 3. The eight river ports earn half province-level GB, keeping half-bars exactly (level 3 pays 1.5 GB). Both rules profiles use these rates. Forecast, settlement, UI and save/load share this rule; previous save flags are upgraded prospectively without rewriting balances or historical reports. This is an explicit HD design choice. The original executable instead excludes icon 95 and gives full-level income to icons 94/96; the help describes half-level income for all ports. See [port-income investigation](PORT_INCOME_INVESTIGATION.md) for the retained original evidence. No paired sea endpoints or historical initial land routes/tributes are fabricated. The app loads this optional sidecar; the original scenario fingerprint, all 206 province IDs, 51 domains, 766 holdings and 18 selection flags remain unchanged.

**Accounting and transfers.** Action expenditure is immediate and journaled separately; a seasonal report opens _after_ those expenditures. Shared pure formulas provide exact expected dice values without changing RNG. The existing scheduler collects once after round three: province/holding/trade receipts, conserved Law Claims, simultaneous reserved tribute transfers, administration/occupation/court/fort/unit expenses, RP collection, delayed loyalty, then existing siege/events/new-season processing. This end-of-season placement and deterministic ordering adapt the original maintenance/collection interface to the existing engine. Reports retain opening/closing GB, actual category totals, unpaid expenses and RP payroll. Forecasts assume solvent tribute payers; actual receipts credit only GB actually paid. Incoming tribute cannot finance a circular obligation in the same settlement.

Gold gifts transfer the stated amount, plus a separately recorded 1 GB/1 RP diplomacy fee and one action. Voluntary fixed-GB seasonal pledges use the same fee/action; no forced vassalage is implemented. Renouncing one's pledge is a free compatibility control. On insolvency, the compatibility priority pays compulsory administration/occupation first, lowers court to an affordable tier, lets unpaid forts decay one level, pays troops in GB or, if necessary, **5 RP/GB**, then disbands unpaid units. Compulsory costs still unaffordable mark bankruptcy and defeat/elimination, consistent with the CD's terminal deficit rule; gold never becomes negative. Automated payment choices/priority replace the original interactive payroll decisions. No arbitrary treasury cap or rescaling of existing balances is imposed: the historical source stores treasury in tenths and has an overflow-prevention cap; the original template's conversion to displayed GB still needs stock verification.

**Explicit approximation: Law Claims.** Disabled by default, enabled in the existing council. The CD specifies 0–3 GB and relative law/asset levels but does not provide its distribution. The modified source contains contradictory, potentially excessive formulas and a dangling `else`. The implementation uses its corrected relative-level allocation, capped at 3 and available revenue, with equal debit/credit. This is a compatibility approximation, not a verified stock probability. Alliance penalties, exact opposition AI for trade, native unit payroll classification and stock accounting/UI timing need further investigation. Other route-disruption/capacity tie-breaking rules are deterministic compatibility policies.

Old saves retain resources and IDs, gain optional economic fields, and load missing routes as **empty**, never as invented routes. Covenant Marches keeps its legacy economy unless a test explicitly selects PC mechanics. AI uses legal trade candidates and reserves funds against expected maintenance shortfalls. M1B-3 introduced no graphics, combat rebalance or recruitment redesign; subsequent magic integration is documented above.

## M1B-2 — Domain rounds and holdings

**Evidence:** CD `RULES.TXT` §§004/040 (actions/free actions), 144 (Agitate), 148 (Contest), 149 (Create Holding), 160 (Rule), and the already inspected scanned manual's printed pp. 7/10, which delegates detailed rules to the in-game help. Source bodies at commit `fc47055002c4b776bfb1e8737b5aaf7f6d43edce`: `MAPAI.CPP::DoNPCAdmin` (seasonal random `RealmOrder`) and `DoNPCAction` (one slot per realm, three `global_action_turn` rounds, then seasonal processing); `ACTNMENU.CPP::IncActionTurn`, `PaintRule` (4314 onward), `PaintCreateHolding` (4691 onward), `PaintContest` (5118 onward), `PaintAgitate` (2235 onward), `RollSuccess`, `CheckAndFixSourceLevels`; and `MULTIMAP.CPP::AskAndWaitForTargetPlayer` (1417 onward). Source files are private research inputs, not compiled/distributed code. The repository is modified historical code, so formula corroboration is distinguished from certification against the retail executable. No tabletop initiative or generic tabletop success formula was substituted.

Anuire now enables the `pc` domain-action policy; Covenant Marches keeps its explicit legacy regression policy. A seeded seasonal order is reused for all three rounds, including holding-only domains. Regular action attempts consume one slot whether they succeed or fail. `Hold action` passes one slot. `End season` passes remaining slots, allowing intervening AI actions and defensive RP responses. Free orders do not advance the cursor. M1B-1 war expiration, occupation, siege and investment run at the actual seasonal boundary. The current round and pending dispute survive save/load.

For level **L**, **b** offensive RP and **g** additional GB, the reconstructed player-facing checks are:

| Action          | Cost                                          | Required d20 before opposition                                     |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Rule Province   | L+1 GB; L+1 RP if L>0, otherwise 0 RP; plus b | 10+L−b                                                             |
| Create Holding  | 1 GB + b RP                                   | 10−province level−b in own province; 10+province level−b elsewhere |
| Rule Holding    | 1 GB; L+1 RP if L>0, otherwise 0 RP; plus b   | 10+L−b                                                             |
| Contest Holding | 0 GB; 1+b RP                                  | 10+target level−attacker's influence−b                             |
| Agitate         | 1+g GB; 1+b RP per target                     | 10+province level−highest own holding+signed sovereign law−b−g     |

Rolls succeed on d20 ≥ the threshold, with minimum threshold 1. Higher-than-20 requirements have zero chance. Costs are committed at submission and remain spent on failure. Province growth stops at 10; preserved higher starting levels are not lowered. Foreign occupation and military contest prevent Rule Province. A sovereign may Rule away political contest, including at the preserved level ceiling. Non-elven growth reduces magical potential and trims source levels to fit; elven population flags preserve source potential. Highest-source-first reduction with stable ID ties implements the apparent source intent rather than reproducing the historical routine's suspicious unchanged `maxl` variable. No extra terrain cap was established in this PC implementation.

Create establishes an independent **level-zero** holding, including in a full pool; a duplicate active owner/type/province holding is disallowed. Law/guild/temple pools are limited by province level; sources by magical potential. Imported excesses survive, but cannot be enlarged. One Rule action can select several holdings, paying and rolling separately. A contested holding can be restored even at capacity; it grows only where capacity and the level-10 ceiling allow. The code does not impose a priest-only temple or wizard-only source establishment restriction; class-dependent regency/magic remains separate.

Contest requires a local holding (zero counts) or sovereignty, as the source UI permits. Influence is province level for its sovereign, otherwise the highest local owned holding/fort level; holding type need not match the target. A first successful contest marks a positive holding contested: its income and regency stop. Success against an already contested or level-zero holding destroys it. Destroyed records remain as zero-level tombstones with their original ID/owner for provenance and save references; they consume no capacity or survival assets. No ownership transfer occurs. A successful Rule clears contest. Zero-level active holdings keep a nonterritorial domain alive.

Agitate changes loyalty by one grade per selected province. Its influence uses the highest own holding (−1 if absent); sovereign law is subtracted for the acting sovereign, added for a rival. Sovereignty or a local holding is required. Priests get one free Agitate action per season, including a batch; GB/RP costs still apply. The command API supports batches; the existing province UI exposes individual raise/lower attempts. Maximum loyalty is 3 unless a higher imported value must be preserved.

Defenders remain independent participants and spend their own RP without using an action. Create targets the sovereign; Contest targets the holding owner. Agitate permits local holding owners and the sovereign to oppose, following CD §144 (the recovered source's target-selection path is narrower). Each opposing RP adds one to the threshold. Offensive RP subtracts one; Agitate also permits GB. Rule uses its distinct difficulty and bids; no general rival RP opposition is invented for Rule, because recovered `AskAndWaitForTargetPlayer` explicitly skips NPC Rule opposition. Rival domains can instead Contest or Agitate.

The player can answer an AI action, or counter an NPC's opposition to their own action, in the existing council. Pending targets, committed checks, opposition, role and cursor are serialized. NPC initial responses retain the recovered action-specific 1/3 Agitate and 1/2 Create/Contest decision branches and RP caps. This is not a universal fixed defense fee.

**Remaining approximations:** initial/newly migrated campaigns rotate the seeded opening order to the player to preserve the untouched imported template/current budget; later seasons use the full random order. All active holding-only domains participate, while original non-landed/vassal scheduling needs further tracing. Movement and battle resolution remain immediate; the original between-round engagement/movement synchronization is not reproduced. Marching is now free of domain actions but still limited to the existing one edge/army/season; original fractional movement charges await M1B-5. RP disputes repeat when either side changes its bid, ending when a side passes or cannot/will not spend more. The exact original AI attack-side base success/spending and difficulty/personality policy, plus special item/dual-class/lieutenant bonuses, remain unresolved; AI uses the same transparent action costs/checks as the player. Batch bids are committed together; partial cancellation between targets is not exposed. Basic AI uses legal checks, keeps garrisons, restores contested holdings and can contest hostile holdings, with a bounded number of free orders per slot. Investiture RP opposition and the separate occupation-screen Reduce Foreign Holdings operation remain deferred. Trade/income reconstruction and realm magic remain later M1B work.

Old save version 1 is extended additively. A migration preserves all balances, records, previous actions and ownership, aligns the player with their remaining round budget, and records the upgrade in the chronicle; completed AI rounds are not retroactively invented. New saves preserve pending disputes and the priest's free-action usage. The Anuire data fingerprint and original availability flags are unchanged.

TypeScript and the final browser production build passed, including the complete original-asset copy.

## M1B-1 — Warfare, occupation and investiture

The restored roadmap places mechanics in M1B; completed graphics/interface work belongs to M1A. No visual redesign accompanies this change.

**Confirmed in the CD help:** `reference/extracted/disc/RULES.TXT`, sections 113/115/117/118, 127, 150/223 and 155. A declaration grants the declaring realm permission to invade; the defender needs its own declaration to counter-invade. Classic wars expire at the seasonal boundary and cannot be declared as the third action. Allied passage does not create occupation. Victory creates military control, not sovereignty. At least one occupying unit must remain. Occupied provinces produce tax for their occupier, no provincial regency for either realm, and cost the occupier an additional 1 GB per season. Holding ownership remains independent. Troops count as law for loyalty protection; initial foreign occupation sets loyalty to poor, with a seasonal occupation penalty subject to law protection.

To invest, clear enemy troops, neutralize hostile forts with units at least equal to their level, and maintain a garrison equal to **province level + law levels** across the third action/first action seasonal boundary without attack. Castles and wizard towers remain separately owned; a sufficient besieging force reduces their level by one per domain turn. Investment does not capture a surviving enemy's castle. The last-province fort exception is applied only to forts belonging to that sovereign in the transferred province; third-party forts remain independent.

**Historical implementation corroboration, not a claim of stock-executable equivalence:** inspected [ACTNMENU.CPP](https://github.com/fire-lizard/birthrt/blob/fc47055002c4b776bfb1e8737b5aaf7f6d43edce/ACTNMENU.CPP#L5666), `PaintInvestiture` (5666–5812), `RollSuccess` (523 onward), `PaintDeclareWar`, and [GAMEMAP.CPP](https://github.com/fire-lizard/birthrt/blob/fc47055002c4b776bfb1e8737b5aaf7f6d43edce/GAMEMAP.CPP#L2058), `ProvinceInfo`, `CastleBesieged`, `CheckEnemyCastle`, `DegradeCastles`, seasonal occupancy recomputation and `CheckRealmStatus`. `MAPAI.CPP::FindProvinceToInvest` was also inspected. Downloads in private `reference/research-cache/m1b1/` were compared by Git blob SHA-1 with the cached tree at commit `fc47055002c4b776bfb1e8737b5aaf7f6d43edce`: all three main files match. Their SHA-256 values are `f1ac640156e111b7d7453c8810aa5c3e2cc4b5525d083c0c7dcac1dbe8b79c12` (ACTNMENU), `5fe513892adec3f577c025d5a9ad19b754dc40df52cd54ffb262cdac6444f1d3` (GAMEMAP), `d8b6040f65b784302f17603132a1ffe3dc40ed8f01dbdbe80f08d4724e24bedf` (MAPAI). No historical implementation is copied into the engine or executed.

That code uses **1 GB with a priest / 4 GB without**, **province level RP + optional bid of 0–9 RP**, one domain action, and a d20 roll **≥ 10 − bid**. Both successful and failed attempts pay. A priest regent or an identified priest deployment belonging to the realm supplies the discount; no mandatory temple holding or invented universal religious prerequisite is imposed. Successful investment changes legitimate ownership and future provincial regency, without moving holdings or transferring treasury/RP reserves. Domain power is recomputed from current assets. The former ruler survives if it retains domain assets. Roll outcomes, occupation changes, retreat, siege and pillage enter the chronicle.

The scanned manual was inspected at printed pp. 7 and 10: it delegates detailed domain-action rules to the in-game help and identifies Investiture among Expert-mode actions. The 1.002 patch notes explicitly fix allied/vassal visits incorrectly causing occupation, and retreat through wards; 1.003 fixes a defeated character failing to retreat. The archived mechanics reference informed section selection, but the CD text and actual source bodies were read directly. No tabletop rule was substituted for these PC rules.

**How to play:** Diplomacy → Declare war; Military → select army/destination → Issue march order. In Provinces, inspect the occupied province: sovereign, occupier, disputed control, fort owners and required garrison are shown separately. Keep sufficient troops through End season. Then use **Investiture**, optionally spending RP to improve the displayed chance. The result updates sovereignty immediately. Failed rolls retain occupation; another attempt requires another action/resources. Tax policy and recruitment are available to an uncontested occupier; province development/fort construction retain the existing sovereign-control restriction. Ordinary independent holding income and improvement remain available; seizure of holdings is not automatic.

**Explicit remaining compatibility rules:**

- M1B-2 now interleaves three regent action rounds. Eligibility still observes a real seasonal boundary with a sufficient garrison; any attack during that season resets readiness, even when repelled. The original engagement delay and weighted movement remain unresolved; movement no longer costs a domain action in Anuire.
- Contested military control blocks provincial taxes, recruitment and investment. Under-strength hostile forts contest control; sufficiently neutralized forts retain their owner. Source `CheckEnemyCastle` marks any hostile castle contested, conflicting with CD help's neutralization-by-unit-count rule; the CD rule takes precedence here. Departure of the last occupier restores the sovereign's control immediately (consistent with source recomputation); CD help's wording about the ruler's units at the end of a turn needs stock-VM verification.
- Law requirement sums existing law levels, minimum one unit. Auto-battle power, terrain bonuses and casualty percentages remain compatibility values. Losers can retain 25% strength and retreat via a deterministic, safe directed border to own/allied control; trapped survivors and units below 25% are lost. Help sections 223 and the tactical survival paragraph disagree about retreat into enemy lands; the stricter safe own/allied route is used. Tactical reserve/combination rules and ward interactions remain pending.
- Pillage is an explicit free economy order, once per province per season: severe tax at min(units, province level), then −1 province level and −1 loyalty. Civilization-dependent holdings shrink to fit while retaining IDs/owners; stable reverse-ID reduction across competing holdings is a compatibility allocation, not a verified original priority. Sources are preserved. The separate Reduce Foreign Holdings operation remains deferred.
- Investiture RP opposition, original lieutenant/dual-class checks, the special investiture blood ability/item modifier, automatic Basic/Advanced-mode investment, worldwide alliance/reputation cascades, and exact retail last-landed-realm destruction are not reproduced. The requested asset-based domain survival overrides historical territorial extermination: independent holdings/domains are never deleted just for losing land. Generic maintenance, economy and recruitment costs remain incomplete pending later M1B stages.
- Enhanced deliberately retains declarations until peace; Classic expires them. Both keep occupation/sovereignty across seasons. Covenant Marches explicitly retains its legacy `conquest: transfer` fixture policy.

Save version 1 is extended additively. Old saves preserve every entity, owner and balance; existing reciprocal war permissions are retained in both directions. Unknown occupation age requires a freshly observed boundary. An upgrade chronicle entry explains this; derived province fort summaries are rebuilt from saved fort owners, without reversing earlier captures or inventing lost history. New saves retain directional declarations, occupation timing, interrupted readiness, military contest status and the last pillage turn. Save fingerprints and imported IDs are unchanged.

**Classic / Enhanced** are rules profiles. Classic expires wars seasonally and prevents a last-action declaration; Enhanced keeps wars until peace. Both retain simplified development, negotiation and combat. War declarations cost one action and no GB/RP; other diplomacy retains its compatibility costs. Neither is a complete reproduction of the 1997 rules.

**CLASSIC / UNRESTRICTED** faction availability is a separate configuration. Original selectability flags are preserved independently of technical support and selection overrides. The current UI exposes 18 eligible regents. Nonterritorial player campaigns, classless rulers and special factions require further investigation; unused records are not assumed playable.

Land trade and 13 realm-spell entries are implemented; tactical battles, five remaining spell entries and RPG adventures remain unfinished. Original save character tails and exact post-New-Game transformations are not reconstructed. No prototype spell is offered in Anuire.
