- Native date after the opening report is 1. HD begins at turn 1; therefore **native event date = HD turn**. First eligible events occur on turn 2, after the first completed season. Previous HD delayed to turn 3 and used turn minus one in formulas.

- The final player-directed scan contains a previously missed bias: its counter decreases for **every province scanned**, not only owned provinces. ## Previous and new behavior

Previous HD had a bounded standard dispatcher, generic logs and seasonal treaty thresholds; it lacked independent event options, explicit difficulty, original reinforcement generators and the Gorgon storyline. It individually gated economic recipients, omitted surplus, chose rebellion's absolute lowest positive loyalty, immediately contested it, and routed military spawns using initial ownership.

The new dispatcher covers all IDs 0–17 with numeric diagnostics and stable keys. Economic grants use the original shared Gorgon gate and time formulas in GB, without visible grant reports. Bless/Blight coexist; counters refresh, ward/dispel replace one another, ward belongs to MONSTERS, and antimagic preserves seeded resistance and the existing additional RP cost. Effects expire at the seasonal boundary **before** report income. Rebellion starts from the randomly selected province and only replaces it on a strictly lower positive loyalty comparison; contesting remains ordinary seasonal work.

Military events use stable native province IDs (including The Gorge, source province 148) and regional geography independent of sovereignty, recovered unit templates, record-based capacity (including characters/reserved slot zero), failed-spawn behavior and the existing 15-record army stacks. Reinforcements reuse the dispatcher without consulting the standard Military Events switch. Gorgon's extra skeleton group follows his actual deployment and the ordinary regional-owner routing, even outside his homeland. Sideath's fixed ward is renewed separately.

The plot preserves 3/8/16 date thresholds, two 1/4 gates, adjusted-score/raw-maximum candidate comparison, adjacency/alliance modifiers, stage-two +10 GB/+50 RP support, and stage-three +40/+20/+10 GB. Stage transitions are exclusive within an invocation. A completed takeover transfers provinces, units, holdings and supported dependent assets, assigns deployed former rulers and lieutenants to Gorgon, and deactivates the former realm without transferring/rescaling its historical cash balance.

Directed diplomatic reactions use the 0x80 grievance condition, 1–5 decreases, ordinary-pair 1/50 increases and special-realm exclusions, plus existing 35/45/40 thresholds and vassal tribute protection. The switch suppresses only random changes. High-balance incidents restore date-dependent parties/theft/embezzlement only when opted in; party help uses recovered forgiveness/reaction behavior. NPC difficulty/date assistance, 20% aid, small reserve conversion/cash fallback, special NPC payroll exemptions and Five Peaks' AI RP exclusion remain independent administration.

Options exposes two event groups, descriptions, eight independent switches, All On/All Off and difficulty in the existing parchment panel. English copy uses the existing shared string catalogue; the application has no other active localization language. Campaign choices are saved independently of local audio/interface preferences. Defaults: seven On, Treasury Incidents Off.

## Reports, assets and save safety

Internal `eventHistory` stores numeric IDs, stable keys, province and applied/disabled/failed outcome, independently of the player journal. Original immediate reports are routed to provincial sovereigns; AI reports do not appear in the player's journal. Imported `STRDAT.DAT` strings (3155/3159/3163/3167/3176/3002/3195) supply original report templates, with retained offsets, string IDs and source hash. The existing English messages remain fallbacks.

The existing importer now incrementally recovers loose `INTRO/GORGON2.FLC` (101 frames), `GORGON3.FLC` (124), `GORGON4.FLC` (128), their palettes, and `TEXT/GORGON1–3.TXT`; complete recovered outputs are reused. Each movie runs at its decoded 71 ms frame interval with source-referenced recovered cues through the existing effects/mute channel. Campaign music is suspended and resumes the same track. Missing movie/text/failed image uses a graceful dispatch fallback. Media and acknowledgements never consume gameplay RNG.

Save v1 defaults are additive; old event phases are not backfilled. Each season is guarded against duplicate event processing. Scene queue and free validated acknowledgement/options commands are saved without spending actions or advancing AI slots. Unknown ally references are rejected on load. Missing/dead/self/player-controlled allies cannot corrupt ownership. Zero/missing allies advance safely without transferring neutral records. No stage-three support or repeated takeover occurs.

## Deliberate HD differences and dependencies

- Retain deterministic serialized HD RNG, independent of rendering/audio, rather than platform `rand()` or multiplayer seed synchronization.
- Keep HD's shared all-realm economy, now split across Maintenance transactions. The current PC path runs standard events and NPC administration on automatic Council dismissal before Collect Taxes; final expenses/RP follow at Select Actions. The initial Council skips the seasonal event/NPC pass. Treasury incidents and other boundary work still occur in `finishSeason` before Council. This supersedes this report's original pre-Maintenance description of income collection before events; exact retail timing remains unverified. No second NPC tax collection is invented. Surplus is a saved AI spending estimate, not an extra payout; the existing legal-action AI remains rather than importing source AI purchase logic.
- Transfer forts, ports, trade routes, ley lines, effect ownership and recruited-lieutenant provenance along with the takeover. Clear the absorbed realm's tribute/war/fealty obligations and recompute derived references. These safeguards adapt source `places[]` and cleanup to HD's separate entities. Existing landed vassals remain separate domains. Duplicate holdings retain identity instead of being silently merged.
- Suppress plot checks/support when Gorgon is eliminated or player-controlled; do not seize an ally that became player-controlled. Do not fabricate a missing regent deployment. Frozen former realm records retain provenance/balances. These are state-integrity safeguards.
- Treaty storage is HD's symmetric kind plus directed reactions, not the source's complete directed status-byte matrix; grievances retain original raw flags separately. RP support is bounded at 65,535. Sideath uses the HD separate ward/resistance fields instead of reproducing the source's overloaded `DispelLvl` storage.
- Opt-in overflow processing is bounded to 100 incidents per report for extreme/malicious balances, records a safety diagnostic and can continue next report. Ordinary balances follow the source loop.
- Tutorial/Basic control bonuses, treasury/prosperity item modifiers, adventure-site selection, multiplayer behavior, tactical battles and RPG adventures require their unfinished subsystems. No substitute flags, fictional item effects or fake adventure sites were added. Original advisor animation/complete report dialogue variants remain presentation work.
