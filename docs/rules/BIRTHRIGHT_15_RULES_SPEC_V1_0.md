## 2. Collections, ownership and exact accounting

Let P be current province level and H the economic level of one owner/type/province holding group. Aggregate the owner's active records before formulas and capacity checks; retain every constituent record and provenance link. Suppressed/destroyed assets do not contribute active levels. Imported excesses are grandfathered, not deleted to satisfy a new capacity rule; they cannot authorize additional illegal expansion.

| Production stream    |               Seasonal GB | Recipient / constraints                                                                                                  |
| -------------------- | ------------------------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Province             | P + approved tax modifier | Legal taxable entitlement under the existing sovereignty/occupation contract, not a second full payment to both parties. |
| Law                  |                       H/3 | Law owner; new production, not an automatic debit from province/Guild/Temple.                                            |
| Guild                |                      2H/3 | Real Guild owner.                                                                                                        |

| Seaport / river port |                   P / P/2 | Existing approved HD port entitlement, with fractional GB preserved.                                                     |

Sources do not consume the real Guild pool. Virtual cash grants no Guild RP, real Guild capacity, trade endpoint, second holding identity or other unapproved virtual-Guild action rights. The latter are outside this contract, not release-blocking decisions. No thief/port/trade RP is added to skill RP. No Law Claims or generic province/holding-count administration charge survives alongside these collections.

**Technical accounting resolution:** use exact integer **1/120-GB ticks** for the 1.5 ledger. This represents the selected thirds, halves, existing tenths and retained infrastructure twelfths exactly. RP remains a whole-point quantity. Use arbitrary-precision integer arithmetic for RP and the new ledger's money quantities: unlimited RP can also be converted into GB, so limiting only the RP field would leave an overflow path. JSON uses canonical decimal integer strings for new quantity fields; BigInt stays behind quantity helpers and serialization adapters. Levels, array indices and die results remain ordinary bounded numbers. This is an engineering contract, not a balance cap or permission to rewrite unrelated systems.

Keep original static-price/source schemas intact; convert their known units at the new ledger boundary. Localize quantity helpers and adapt existing quotes/reports incrementally. Do not switch a campaign to 1.5 while a participating command still projects an unbounded balance through a JavaScript Number. This avoids a framework rewrite while requiring complete numeric coverage of the selected rules path.

Do not quantize ordinary receipts per original holding record. For a proportional transfer that cannot divide into whole ticks, allocate whole ticks by largest remainder, ties by stable asset ID; transferred debits must sum exactly to credits. Legal bid increments remain those of the action, not an invented 1/120-GB influence purchase. Displays may round for readability but must expose exact ledger values and never feed rounded values back into calculations.

Ledger entries identify season/boundary, stream, owner, source assets, counterparty, gross obligation, waiver, payment, unfulfilled balance and resulting consequence. Production, transfers, RP→GB Alchemy and RP-funded payroll have different entry types. An obligation reservation is not income. Each source/stream/boundary has one receipt key; a transfer has matching debit and credit. Forecasts and rejected commands consume no gameplay RNG.

## 4. V01 evaluation — preserved-score revised B35 with unlimited stock

### 4.1 Preferred candidate, pending approval

Let **S equal Sierra's preserved numerical bloodline A** on conversion; keep derivation, grade and abilities separate. Subsequent earned changes affect a derived current S, never the raw source field. Odd scores remain odd. Do not halve A, double A, use the grade as S, or add character level to the cap.

```text
Q = floor(sum of skill-qualified, type-eligible controlled levels)
seasonal collected RP = min(Q, current S) for an eligible blooded ruler
closing RP = opening RP + actual collections + transfers + authorized sacrifice
             - committed RP spending - approved growth expenditure
RP storage limit = NONE
```

There is no `reserveRoom`, `min(stock, 2S/5S/65535)`, reduced replenishment above an opening floor, expiry, annual stock reset or saturating arithmetic. A reserve of 1,000 RP with S=30 and Q=12 collects 12 more, before spending. A reserve of 50 at S=0 is preserved; ordinary collection and wise-rule growth do not become available merely because that stock exists.

Use revised B35 chapter 2's growth/sacrifice prices as a coherent pair:

- **Growth +1 S costs S+1 RP.** Historical eligibility requires the reserve to **exceed** that price at two successive seasonal boundaries, with at most two points/year. For integer RP, qualifying surplus is at least S+2. Test spendable RP after current obligations/reservations, never unpaid commitments or forecast receipts. Record the two-boundary streak; reset it after growth, sacrifice, ruler replacement or an intervening failure. One increase consumes its price, not the whole reserve. Calendar year is `floor((seasonIndex−1)/4)`; two increases/year is a limit on this wise-rule method, not a new RP cap.
- **Sacrifice −1 S yields the previous S RP.** Reprice after every point lost: sacrificing k points from S yields `k(2S−k+1)/2`, with 0≤k≤S. It never runs silently in payroll, bids, casting or defaults. Reaching zero removes blooded collection eligibility; no negative bloodline or automatic unblooded→blooded purchase is allowed.
- **Recommended 1.5 management adaptation:** growth is an eligible funded choice/policy rather than a forced invisible debit. Basic may execute the selected automatic growth policy; Advanced presents the funded proposal; Expert issues the order. All use the same eligibility/cost/calendar. Sacrifice always needs an explicit named authorization/policy. This elective treatment and the streak-reset convention are part of V01's proposed package, not claims of verbatim history.

Revised B35 ch. 2 PDF pp. 8–9 supplies S collection, S+1 growth, previous-S sacrifice and timing. **S=A is a 1.5 conversion choice; unlimited stock is the explicit V14 enhancement.** That historical chapter also has a 2S stock cap, which is rejected. Older chapter 5's 2B/5B and doubled sacrifice are not mixed into this package. The literal historical growth text is automatic; the elective adaptation above needs V01 approval along with the formula.

## 6. Trade, Source handlers and ley networks

Each route has one canonical contract ID, two positive uncontested real Guild groups, consent, endpoint owners, capacity reservations, transport/infrastructure payer and two explicit income shares. Reject reversed duplicates and record-splitting capacity tricks. Level 0 and virtual Guilds are not profit endpoints. An owner at both ends receives H_a/2+H_b/2, not two full route totals. A vessel/cargo reservation cannot also serve another route or troop transport. No initial ships, routes, road networks, salaries or consents are invented from their absence in decoded records. Do not add an arbitrary per-cast RP ceiling as a substitute for unlimited-stock approval.

## 8. Seasonal transaction and management modes

Preserve the existing action-round/season flow. The following is the 1.5 settlement dependency order, not a new claim about Sierra's exact order of play:

1. Finish accepted round orders and their reserved costs. Freeze collection rights, lawful ownership/control, tax modifiers, qualified handlers, trade/transport contracts and promised spell collections. Effects with one collection left remain eligible for it.

Credit skill/bloodline-qualified RP once after V01 approval; settle paid RP pledges once. Orders reserve resources; the settlement never charges an already-paid order again. Sequential event handlers must annotate their effective boundary rather than replay collection on load. Random checks are committed once using the serialized PRNG and a persisted cursor/receipt. ### V01 — Score, collection and wise-rule growth

### F01 — Court seat and palace interpretation

**A — Recommended:** C is the paid/effective court used for action brackets. Maximum C is seat-province P + funded palace L; a palace adds capacity, **not another free L to paid C**. Palace capital is 6 GB/level and upkeep L/2 GB. A new palace level requires a paid Build court/standard opportunity and completes no earlier than the next round; +1 court/season still applies. A seat needs an owned province or a real positive holding plus host permission. Grandfather opening court 1 while its archival seat is unspecified; require a reviewed seat before increases. New court upkeep applies at the forthcoming boundary, with extra slots beginning next round; no retroactive or same-round slot minting. Permit one named lead actor/full-action rank bonus per check, not stacked lieutenant bonuses.

**B:** implement the additional palace-effective-level bonus as well as capacity. This grants more actions for the same court upkeep and requires repricing the affected developed-court cases. A is recommended because the approved court-price/throughput table remains the actual paid table and no missing initial palace is invented.

### F02 — Tax attitude and bounded Seizure execution

**A — Recommended completion package:** one 1-GB tax Decree/domain/season changes the persistent modifier by ±1 for a declared set of owned provinces; each must remain between zero receipts and P+floor(P/2). A province does not get a second modification that season. This matches the historical realm-wide example without allowing one fee per court slot to bypass the seasonal limit. Replace old tax-grade effects with one Sierra-style five-grade loyalty update: `taxDelta = -persistent modifier`, occupation contributes −1, and existing Law/garrison coercion can cancel at most two negative steps (full P control) or one (at least half P); positive changes are not multiplied. Agitate/spells/events contribute their once-only explicit grade changes; clamp the resulting grade to the existing 0–4 range. This direct modifier-to-grade adaptation is intentional P15, not the BRCS d20 attitude table. Using only the modifier's sign would make +1 and +5 GB equally unpopular and encourage automatic maximum taxation; A avoids that loophole.

Seizure: one standard action +1 GB per acting Law-owner/province group/season, positive uncontested Law required; d20 vs DC 10 + largest targeted active asset level, +acting Law H and eligible committed RP support, −typed opposition/defensive RP. All affected owners receive one response window; no automatic GB-buying modifier. On success roll one d6 and book `min(d6, Law H, eligible victim receipts)` for the next collection. Target actual third-party province/Guild/Temple receipts, excluding already committed receipts, Sources, RP, virtual cash, trade/Alchemy and the actor's own assets. Proportionally debit the selected eligible receipts; all rival Seizures share the same reserved province/victim pools. Fortified institutions are immune only when individually linked to a separately supported protecting asset of at least their H and not breached; a province castle does not blanket-protect everything. Successful Seizure imposes −1 provincial loyalty if the actor is sovereign, plus one worsening of the existing diplomatic relation with each victim, once/acting domain/province/season. For a non-sovereign actor, record local hostility imposing −1 on its political domain checks there during the next season instead of reducing loyalty to someone else. These consequences do not generate another copy of lost cash. Reserve the quoted target/pool at order resolution; at collection pay at most its still-eligible receipts, without rerolling or substituting a new victim.

**B:** retain A's deterministic attitude and conserved Seizure contract, but allow only one target province per seasonal tax Decree instead of a declared province set. This slows broad tax changes without changing the approved ±1 steps, 50% limit or paid action. A is recommended because the inspected BRCS example changes several provinces together, and it keeps a single coherent attitude formula without silently correcting the disputed BRCS probability table. Neither alternative disables the already approved taxation or Seizure feature.

### F03 — Contest timing, occupation pressure and fort counterplay

**A — Recommended completion package:** keep current political Contest price **1 RP + bids and one standard opportunity**, with no added BRCS 1d3 damage. Eligible attacker has an active same-type holding; active Law may attack Guild/Temple; Sources require eligible Source/mage competition. Use DC 10+target H, +eligible acting typed holding level, typed support/opposition and paid RP bids. Sovereignty alone does not substitute for a Source. Stage one suppresses all economic/RP/casting/route support of the target; the earliest destroying success is a **later round**, after an owner response opportunity. Restoring a suppressed holding uses one standard opportunity, 1 GB and DC 10+H, no level increase. Newborn level-0 contacts are removed by one successful Contest; suppression otherwise persists until restoration/destruction, not automatic expiry. Political Contest can reach an institution protected from physical seizure; fort upkeep does not buy absolute political immunity.

For military suppression, require continuous funded occupation for a full round/month with no interrupted control, enemy battle or unresolved hostile fort; commit one standard Occupy/suppression order costing 1 GB/province. At resolution choose at most one unprotected mundane holding group; each stationed funded military unit supports at most one suppressed holding level, never more than target H. This temporarily removes that many economic levels. A later full paid round may destroy **one already suppressed level**, using a separately paid order; repeat only after another funded round. No mass reset of unrelated institutions; zero-level contacts may be removed as that target. Release of the funded force restores temporary military suppression prospectively, but does not reverse actually destroyed levels or political Contest. Sources remain immune to generic soldiers. The order fee is separate from existing foreign-occupation upkeep (1 GB/province/season), unit payroll and fort/siege costs; refusal/inability to maintain the order ends its suppression benefit and preparation, without transferring legal title.

**B:** retain the current longer investiture-style season boundary as the minimum occupation preparation instead of one round; keep the same paid target/strength limits. Slower military institutional pressure, simpler reuse of existing preparation. A provides bounded military pressure and fort counterplay while keeping legal ownership separate. This does not shorten the existing garrison/investiture requirements or change tactical combat.

**Approval boundary:** V01/F01/F02/F03 are the entire remaining rules questionnaire for this implementation contract. A chosen package's explicit clauses can be amended by the user, but no implementer may silently label them technical fixes. Unselected optional expansions are excluded rather than accumulated as new pre-coding audits. Approving a package still does not authorize gameplay edits in this documentation step.
