## Activation and scheduler boundary

Initialization requires explicit1.5 rules, the exact installed1.2.0 overlay pair, initialized Increment4 accounts, and an **unused seasonal action-phase checkpoint**. It rejects Council/Maintenance, pending legacy disputes and partial-round/previously spent budgets whose personal/Court usage cannot be reconstructed. It never dismisses Council, executes income, pays general Maintenance, initializes past receipts or converts a save. The validation script labels its fresh derived checkpoint explicitly; it does not claim that real settlement occurred.

The existing `domainTurn` remains the sole round/order/cursor. Its seeded seasonal order and three-round helper are reused. `end-slot` explicitly passes the current domain's unused standard opportunity and Court slots; it never runs AI, another command, a collection or a season transition. At the end of round3 it parks the valid existing cursor and records `awaitingMaintenance`. Further orders are blocked. The future orchestrator must close the prior RP boundary and actually advance the campaign season before `resume-season` can initialize the next three-round schedule. That request does not advance `turn` or run Maintenance itself.

General `applyCommand` guards remain. Explicit requests use the same Basic selected-policy / Advanced confirmation / Expert order authorization forms as Increment4; no mode UI or automatic management policy is enabled.

## Court, lawful seats and palaces

Court upkeep is C GB/season. Extra Court actions per round are0 at C0–2,1 at C3–4,2 at C5,3 at C6–7,4 at C8–9 and5 at C10+. Court0 still permits the ordinary standard action. The derived court section separates selected level, funded support, capacity, delayed action level and upkeep applicability; the archival legacy court tier is preserved.

Effective Court is bounded by a lawful seat's current province level plus completed funded palace levels. An owned province is lawful. A foreign seat additionally requires an actual positive uncontested holding and the current sovereign's explicit saved consent. Mere holding ownership, a candidate location or an alliance does not grant permission. Host consent can be revoked; lost permission or hostile control removes unsupported effective capacity. Opening Court1 with no historical seat is grandfathered, but cannot be increased without an explicitly reviewed legal seat. No opening palace or historical seat is invented.

Palace Build costs6GB per level and uses an actual standard/full opportunity or Court slot. A standard Build also reserves its named actor's personal opportunity. Construction completes only at the next global action round. The runtime palace ID/owner/location remain stable across extensions; original assets are not replaced. **Capital does not pay upkeep:** completed levels supply capacity only after their actual L/2GB seasonal support is funded. A round3 project cannot provide capacity in round3 and requires support after completion. Funding a later level charges only its unfunded half-GB difference; already funded levels remain individually supported. The full known upkeep remains in forecasts, while only the actually funded portion qualifies for Administrate. Funding cannot be paid twice; an unavailable/unfunded level contributes no capacity. Separate court/palace funding primitives book real upkeep through Increment4 and expose identities for later once-only settlement; they do not constitute general Maintenance.

Court increase costs a1GB Decree and one legal standard/full or Court opportunity. It requires a lawful seat and supported capacity, and can occur at most once/domain/season. Extra slots start **next round**; the new upkeep level starts **next season's eligible obligation boundary**. A round3 increase cannot mint a current-season slot. Palaces increase capacity only, never the action bracket directly. All active/used slots are deducted before presenting remaining opportunities.

## Actors, opportunities and Administrate

Every order records domain, stable character ID, reviewed authorization, season/round, exact targets, standard/personal/Court source opportunities and financial links. One standard opportunity/domain/round and one personal opportunity/original character/round are authoritative across every pending and completed order. An uncommitted cancellation releases holds; a committed failure does not. Court actions use actual Court slots and do not become extra standard actions. Only a standard/full action receives the named actor's personal rank assistance. No skill rank supplies extra actions.

Lieutenants require a unique real deployment, an explicit role/appointment, reviewed character content and a matching reviewed authority context in the pinned overlay. The inventory and conditional candidate lists alone grant nothing. `appoint` records an actual appointment for such a deployed, reviewed candidate; it does not recruit an unidentified person, manufacture a deployment, silently move an independent sovereign into a second office, or add a handler permission. Missing authority/deployment evidence remains an explicit content constraint. Independent vassals retain their own accounts/actions; an assimilated domain has no independent Court or standard budget. Lieutenants spend only their authorizing domain's real account.

Create Holding, Rule Holding/Realm Rule and Rule Province use **Administrate purchased ranks**, as identified by the retained BRCS action headings. Personal assistance is `floor(ranks/5)` once per check, from one actor. It excludes Wisdom, feats, synergies, equipment and a second lieutenant. Court-only Build/Decree receives no personal bonus. The existing character selectors remain authoritative; no ranks or character sheets are authored here.

Administrate nomination is a separate **pending seasonal handoff**, not a roll or a paid action discount. One named eligible actor/domain/season is recorded before the forthcoming snapshot; the same original person cannot be nominated for multiple domains that season. Current availability is checked. Nomination neither creates nor spends a standard/Court action; it does not reserve every future round. Availability and actual appointment must be rechecked when Increment8 executes the pending seasonal operation. There is no substitution/retry path. The stable handoff key identifies that one future check; its status remains `pending-maintenance` in this increment.

The pure nomination quote reuses Increment3: `d20 + actual Administrate check modifier` against exactly10GB plus gross eligible infrastructure, with `floor(eligibleGB/4)` whole GB possible saving. Supported separately charged forts, funded palace infrastructure and explicitly represented paid roads/bridges qualify. Courts, troops, taxes, ordinary ports, action fees, embedded administration, magic and hypothetical/unclassified infrastructure do not. No die is rolled or discount applied here. Increment8 must freeze eligible obligations/actor, record one die/result and consume the handoff exactly once in the existing resumable Maintenance ledger.

## Development, responses and failure costs

| Order           | Price / check                                                                                               | Committed result                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Create Holding0 | 1GB, DC10, separately authorized RP bid                                                                     | Creates one new runtime contact at0 on success; active equivalent groups cannot be duplicated                                                               |
| Rule Holding    | 1GB, DC10+aggregated current H, bid                                                                         | +1 logical group level on success; no automatic own-H bonus or fixed target-level RP charge                                                                 |
| Realm Rule      | One standard/full action plus one Court slot/additional province; each target separately priced/bid/checked | One host sovereignty, one holding type, distinct finite provinces; successes/failures apply individually with no refund of failed attempts                  |
| Rule Province   | Current P GB, DC10+P, no RP or holding modifiers                                                            | One attempt/domain/season even on failure, another actor or another province; legal sovereignty, hostile-control and existing development ceilings enforced |

Constituent holding records remain intact. Costs/DCs/capacity use the aggregated owner/type/province group. A successful Rule increments exactly one writable constituent, selected by stable ID; it never replaces, merges or renumbers original records. Imported excess cannot authorize expansion. Political restoration is explicitly deferred to Increment7 rather than silently turning growth into restoration. Source development/competition requires an actually reviewed true-arcane actor; Spider's economic virtual handler exception alone does not establish that right.

Typed influence is explicitly selected, not automatically added. Same-type groups can support/oppose; Law can affect Guild/Temple, never generic Source checks. The ruled group cannot supply its own H through another raw constituent. A group is counted once per target, a responder contributes once, and Source responses require the actual qualified mage plus legitimate Source presence. A real Source0 contact can permit a qualified mage's RP response while supplying zero typed levels. Mundane local RP participation is likewise distinct from typed modifiers. Each deterministic response queue includes eligible domains and requires an explicit authorized response/pass, including NPCs in this headless path. No synthetic NPC bid strength or extra random AI policy is introduced.

The initial bid, every response bid and each target fee use real exact Increment4 reservations, commitment and payment. Bids are whole unbounded RP, not GB influence purchases. The final required die is `DC − personal bonus − offensive bid − signed typed/RP contributions`, using BigInt until comparison with the seeded d20. There is no maximum-skill automatic-success rule. A large lawful modifier can make an ordinary roll sufficient, just as the standard threshold permits. Failed checks keep their paid costs and consumed opportunities.

One globally committed order owns the current response window. A batch resolves one target at a time; its exact index, responses and results survive reload. Unrelated action/seat/funding changes wait until that response window finishes. A completed target or order cannot be rolled again under another transaction ID. Realm Rule cannot reopen a batch, duplicate a province through fragmented holdings, obtain a free unfunded target or create additional independent magic casts.

Province success updates population and uses the existing terrain/geographic/elven `sourcePotential` selector. Source potential, levels and ownership remain distinct. Elven exceptions preserve potential; no universal new subtraction formula is added. The existing grandfather-capacity policy preserves imported Source levels. Only later-developed excess above that permitted pool is reduced when the recovered potential actually falls, using the existing highest-level/stable-ID convention. Source records, ownership and IDs are never destroyed or transferred by population growth.

## Persistence, payments and frozen collection

The optional `courtActions15.version=1` is additive to the exact version2 save. Existing foundations without it stay uninitialized. Existing1.0/1.1 overlay saves retain their exact content and prior boundaries; Increment5 explicitly requires the installed1.2 pair. No overlay version/hash or rules contract is bumped.

The section stores the starting development entities/authority/scheduler checkpoint, ordered typed requests and a validated projection: court/seat/funding history, completed/pending palace levels, appointments, opportunities, orders/checks, end-slot markers and Administrate nominations. This is a bounded before-state for validating changed development entities, not another account ledger or game engine. Later implementations must extend these typed transitions/hand-offs for their world effects rather than mutate these entities behind the journal.

Each court event records its precise position in the existing RP journal. Replay uses that journal's original prefix view to distinguish past snapshots from a later open boundary. Every expected financial transition must appear in order with its exact payer, obligation, bid, fee and status. The reserved `inc5-` namespace cannot be altered by an unrelated RP request. No second RP account, stock ceiling, relay allowance or resource conversion is introduced. Existing known obligations and same-boundary anti-relay restrictions still apply.

Load/execution validates requests, projection, financial links, original character/overlay authority, stable entity effects, scheduler, RNG and ID sequence. Duplicate transactions, overlapping actor/standard/Court holds, malformed quantities, forged prices/action credits/results, changed payers, replayed rolls and stale quotes fail explicitly. Serialization uses the existing canonical integer-string codecs; semantic replay is mandatory when loading with the pinned overlays. Replay verifies data and never issues a new payment, collection or roll to the live campaign.

Development changes only the next collection not yet frozen. Existing open Increment4 quote snapshots and completed receipts remain untouched; a newly opened quote sees the new legitimate assets. Court/palace paid identities and existing all-GB forecast comparison columns remain distinct. Increment8 must consume paid coverage/commitment identities when billing, as it already must for recorded RP payroll, rather than charging again.
